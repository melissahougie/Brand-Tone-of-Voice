# rePurpose Global Becomes First Advocate Partner in 1% for the Planet's Global Network

### New partnership gives 1% for the Planet's member businesses a direct path to fund verified plastic recovery, with a dual-certification model launching in September 2026

**[CITY, STATE] — [DATE], 2026 —** rePurpose Global, the packaging sustainability and compliance platform behind more than 110 million pounds of verified plastic waste recovery worldwide, today announced it has joined 1% for the Planet as an Impact Partner and is the first organization added to the network's Advocate Program. The designation makes rePurpose Global's Verified Plastic Recovery Programs directly available to 1% for the Planet's global membership of businesses that already commit 1% of annual sales to environmental causes.

The partnership addresses a gap many of those businesses have faced: a straightforward, verified way to direct environmental giving toward measurable plastic recovery, backed by data rather than general claims. Beginning in September 2026, rePurpose Global and 1% for the Planet will introduce a dual-certification model that lets a single contribution qualify for both organizations' recognition at once.

Under the new model:

1. Member businesses direct their 1% for the Planet giving toward rePurpose Global's Verified Plastic Recovery Program.
2. That one contribution earns both 1% for the Planet certification and rePurpose Global's Certified Plastic Recovery credential.
3. Businesses can turn the investment into shelf-visible, on-pack claims tied to measurable, verified plastic recovery data, along with ESG reporting.
4. Both organizations provide co-branding and PR support to amplify the resulting story.

Consumer demand is a major driver behind the move: according to a rePurpose Global internal survey, 95% of U.S. consumers say they would pay more for a product that funds verified plastic recovery. The partnership gives 1% for the Planet's business network a direct way to act on that demand while strengthening the credibility of their environmental claims.

rePurpose Global's Verified Plastic Recovery Programs currently span five countries and counting, including India, Indonesia, Colombia, Cameroon, and Kenya, where the organization works to strengthen local waste management systems and create dignified livelihoods for waste workers. To date, rePurpose Global has enabled the recovery of more than 110 million pounds of plastic waste through these verified projects.

rePurpose Global already shares several joint customers with 1% for the Planet's broader membership, including Saie, MegaFood, and Nécessaire.
