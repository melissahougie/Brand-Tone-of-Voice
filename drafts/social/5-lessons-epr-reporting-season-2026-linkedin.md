Thousands of EPR reports. One reporting season. A lot of quiet mistakes we didn't expect.

June 1 was the first consolidated EPR deadline under CAA, covering six states and up to eight separate reports depending on where you sell. It was our biggest reporting season yet, and we just spent days with our compliance, data, and product teams picking apart everything that surfaced across all of it.

A few things we saw:
- "Am I obligated?" is five separate questions, not one, and most brands have only answered the first.
- A spreadsheet can look "final" and still be hiding a five- or six-figure classification error.
- One brand handled reporting fully in-house, felt confident about it, and had quietly filed the wrong year's data for two states without realizing.

None of it was negligence. It's what happens when a brand-new, multi-state system launches and everyone's building the muscle at the same time.
