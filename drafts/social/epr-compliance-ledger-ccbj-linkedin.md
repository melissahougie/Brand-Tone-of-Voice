Packaging used to live in marketing, procurement, and ops. EPR is moving it onto legal's calendar.

Our Director of Product, Alyssa Dizon, broke down why for Corporate Counsel Business Journal: with 7 states now running packaging EPR programs, packaging has become regulated business data, complete with ownership questions, documentation requirements, and fee exposure.

A few specifics from the piece:
- Clear PET in a bottle and white PET in a jug get reported differently, and that distinction shows up directly in the fee calculation.
- The obligation hierarchy usually lands on whoever's logo is on the package, not wherever it was manufactured.
- Spreadsheets aren't breaking because they can't hold the data. They're breaking because fragmented data is hard to govern, update, and defend under scrutiny.

Worth a read for any legal team that still thinks of EPR as someone else's problem 👇
