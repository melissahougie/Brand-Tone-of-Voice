Summary
This Loom explains the packaging simulator prototype for customers with the simulator hub enabled, including how brand and supplier users will simulate and project EPR impacts. Viewers start on the insights dashboard, sort packages by EPR fees by default, then simulate an entire package whose components are copied into a draft package where changes automatically trigger tags like component elimination and lightweighting. The projections table shows baseline comparisons and year over year estimates up to 2032, including California source production 2023, with a sales-weighted or units toggle for brand customers and a 5% default year over year growth that can be adjusted. It also covers group simulations that combine multiple simulations without duplicating products, include a toggle for changed versus unchanged products, and produce a Source Reduction Final reduction report via finalize plan, with recyclability and compostability mappings and EPR category status shown for California.
