EPR 101: A Practical Catch-Up Plan for Companies That Feel Behind

Positioning
Much of today's packaging EPR conversation is aimed at companies already deep into the work: source-reduction plans, forecasting, fee modeling, material optimization, and reporting systems.
But many businesses are still earlier in the journey. Up to four in ten estimated fully liable producers may still need to act, while nearly 8,000 smaller producers must register, document their exemption, and prepare for growth and future reporting liabilities.  They may be unsure whether they are obligated, unclear on where to begin, or struggling to assemble even the basic packaging and supplier data needed to make decisions.
Exempt does not mean invisible
Small producers still need to register, apply, document their eligibility, and maintain that registration.
Growth changes the compliance equation
New distribution, retail wins, acquisitions, or rapid California sales growth can move a company across the threshold.
The data cannot be reconstructed overnight
Emerging brands should begin tracking California sales, producer responsibility by brand, packaging components, material types, weights, and source-reduction changes before full reporting becomes mandatory.

Webinar concept
Join Lowell Huffman, Director of Channel Partnerships, and Manika Doshi, CEO of rePurpose Global, for a practical EPR 101 crash course designed for companies that need to get oriented, get organized, and start moving.
Rather than assuming attendees already have a mature compliance program, the session will clarify what EPR means, who may be responsible, what information companies need, and how to establish a realistic path forward.
Manika will also share how rePurpose is building technology and resources to help companies move from uncertainty to action…without treating EPR as a one-time reporting exercise.
