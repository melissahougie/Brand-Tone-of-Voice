🎉 We're excited to announce the first addition to our Advocate Program: rePurpose Global!

rePurpose Global is a leading EPR packaging compliance and sustainability platform, helping 400+ brands take action on plastic waste and another 100+ stay ahead of evolving regulations. Their Impact Projects have collectively recovered more than 100 million pounds of plastic waste, improved the lives of thousands of marginalized waste workers and delivered waste management services to millions of people. On the packaging side, their platform streamlines reporting across all states, enables teams to stay EPR compliant and creates more sustainable packaging without the operational burden.

What does it mean to be an advocate? This newly launched program recognizes organizations committed to growing our movement and accelerating impact by referring new companies to join our community of members.
