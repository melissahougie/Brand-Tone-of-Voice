## What is Plastic Offsetting?

[PARAPHRASED — not verbatim, reconstructed from search summaries]
Plastic offsetting allows organizations to balance out their plastic footprint by funding initiatives that remove, recycle, or repurpose an equivalent amount of plastic waste.

## How It Works — The Three-Step Process

[PARAPHRASED — not verbatim, reconstructed from search summaries]
The process begins with a measurement step using an assessment tool to calculate plastic footprints, fundamentally calculating the weight of plastic used in products and multiplying this by the volume of sales.

Combined with accurate measurement, plastic credits enable businesses to offset their plastic footprint and go "Plastic Neutral" by eliminating as much plastic waste from nature as they use.

## Key Principles from rePurpose Global

[PARAPHRASED — not verbatim, reconstructed from search summaries]
Low-value plastic like to-go containers, candy wrappers, and plastic bags dominate the litter in developing countries, and these materials are classified as low-value plastic because they are extremely difficult to recycle.

Through plastic credit contributions, rePurpose puts a price on low-value plastic and pays informal workers to intercept it before it reaches the oceans or landfills, adding a crucial income stream for these marginalized workers.

## Impact and Certification

[PARAPHRASED — not verbatim, reconstructed from search summaries]
Businesses that purchase plastic credits equivalent to the removal or recycling of plastic waste they generate each year — alongside a commitment to reduce plastic use — are issued rePurpose's "Plastic Neutral Certification," with a requirement that certifications are not a license to continue using plastic.
