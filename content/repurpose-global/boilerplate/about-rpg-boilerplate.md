rePurpose Global is the leading Packaging Sustainability & Compliance Platform for consumer companies. Through its Verified Plastic Recovery Program, rePurpose Global has enabled the recovery of more than 110 million pounds of plastic waste from projects across India, Indonesia, Colombia, Cameroon, Kenya, and beyond, strengthening waste management systems and creating dignified livelihoods for waste workers along the way. Every pound is verified, giving brands a credible way to back their sustainability claims with real, measurable impact instead of a vague promise. Learn more at repurpose.global.
