## NOT GREAT — Example 1

**Post (rePurpose Global, LinkedIn, 47,203 followers):**

> This June, rePurpose hit the road! 5 events. 4 cities. Home furnishings, personal care, packaging, and source reduction — we spent the month in rooms full of people working through the same question: what does EPR actually require of us, and how do we get there?
>
> One thing came up everywhere: most companies feel behind. And almost every time, the reality was the same: the whole field is earlier than people think.
>
> Thanks to our partners for hosting: American Home Furnishings Alliance 2026 Packaging Forum, Baby Safety Alliance (JPMA) Summit, Personal Care Products Council Legal & Regulatory Conference, Circle (formerly OPLN) | The Circular Leadership Network Source Reduction Summit, AMERIPEN 2026 Annual Summit.
>
> Swipe to see a few snaps of our team on the road!

**Feedback:**
- **Generic / Not Thoughtful:**
  - "We went to 5 events and our best/new insight is that companies feel behind" — the actual insight is too shallow to justify the setup.
  - "The whole field is earlier than people think" is unclear — a general statement with no explanation of what it means or why it's true.
- **Judgment:** generalizing all brands as being behind, without providing any helpful insight to go with it.

## NOT GREAT — Example 2

**Post (rePurpose Global, LinkedIn, 47,214 followers):**

> Are you ready for the August 1 deadline?
>
> Most brands aren't, which is why we're hosting our next Office Hours session at the 2-week countdown mark.
>
> Office Hours: Your ISR Plan: The 2-Week Countdown
> 🗓 July 13 · 12:00PM ET
>
> Lowell and Alyssa will be live, sharing insights and answering questions openly and candidly. No slides. No scripted answers. Just the experts who know this best, helping you get across the finish line.
>
> Come off mute, drop questions in the chat, or just listen in. Whatever works for you.
>
> Register to save your spot at the link in comments.

(Featured: Lowell Huffman, Director, Channel Partnerships, rePurpose)

**Feedback:**
- **Generic:** instead of just explaining what Office Hours *are* (answering questions, no scripted answers), the opportunity was to be more specific about the *types* of topics and tough questions rePurpose can actually answer, because of its expertise. Describing the format isn't the same as demonstrating the expertise.

## BETTER

**Post (rePurpose Global, LinkedIn, 47,203 followers):**

> 47 days to your ISR Plan deadline. No pressure, but also some pressure.
>
> ✈️ Source reduction is the next requirement on the runway — and it comes with a binding plan due August 1, 2026.
>
> Get your boarding pass in the latest edition of the Packaging Sustainability & Compliance Digest, where we cover the full picture: what ISR actually requires, how the 5 pathways work, what to avoid, and the resources to get you there.
>
> Read it before your gate closes 👇

**Feedback:**
- Puts pressure, but lightly, with humor and no judgment (versus just calling people out).
- Has specificity: of deadlines, of days, of what we will cover.

## Takeaways for the guide

1. **Light humor and an extended metaphor are approved for social/LinkedIn content specifically** — the flight/runway/boarding-pass conceit in the "Better" post is explicitly praised, not flagged. This refines (not contradicts) the existing "don't overcorrect into casual/jokey" guidance: the metaphor is fine *because* it still carries "47 days," "August 1, 2026," "5 pathways," and a named deliverable. Humor is the delivery mechanism, not a replacement for the specifics.
2. **A stated observation about "most brands"/"the industry" needs either a real explanation or needs to be cut.** "Most companies feel behind" and "the whole field is earlier than people think" were both flagged as generic *and* as unhelpfully judgmental, even though neither line is aggressive in tone. Flat, unexplained generalizations about brands' collective state read as judgment by default — they need a specific, actionable "here's what that means for you" attached, or they shouldn't run.
3. **For event/webinar promo copy: name the specific expertise, don't describe the format.** "No slides, no scripted answers, come off mute or just listen in" describes logistics. What was missing: the actual hard questions rePurpose can uniquely answer. Specificity of content beats specificity of format.
