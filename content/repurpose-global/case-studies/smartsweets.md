SmartSweets built their brand on the promise to "Kick Sugar, Keep Candy," and in 2025 partnered with rePurpose Global to fund plastic recovery.

The Plastic Recovery Pledge: through their Plastic Recovery Pledge with rePurpose Global, SmartSweets commits to removing thousands of pounds of plastic waste every year, taking responsibility for their environmental footprint and working toward resolving the plastic pollution crisis. More specifically, SmartSweets funds the recovery of 200,000 pounds of plastic waste from hotspot pollution areas annually, helping intercept plastic waste before it can enter oceans or ecosystems while also supporting waste workers in regions without reliable waste management infrastructure.

Community impact: at one impact project site, Nuevo Ciclo in Colombia, recovered waste is turned into lumber, creating schools and playgrounds for the surrounding community.

Business benefits: SmartSweets' commitment to plastic recovery has helped position the brand as a leader in sustainable snacking, giving them a credible story to share with consumers, retail buyers, and investors. By aligning environmental action with their mission to create cleaner candy, SmartSweets has strengthened brand loyalty with sustainability-minded shoppers and reinforced its role as a values-first challenger brand.
