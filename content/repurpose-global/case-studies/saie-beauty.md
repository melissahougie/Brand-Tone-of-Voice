Saie is a beauty brand built on feeling good and doing good, with a mission to make clean, award-winning beauty products that are as luxurious and high-performing as they are sustainable.

Partnership overview: Saie partners with rePurpose Global to certify their products Plastic Negative. Saie initially partnered with rePurpose Global to go Plastic Negative and to improve the sustainability of their product lines with packaging Life Cycle Analysis (LCA).

The Saie Climate Initiative: accelerates Saie's sustainability goals across all areas of its processes, from product development to plastic recovery. The triple-pronged approach strives to clean the planet, go carbon net zero across emission scopes, and invest in female sustainability expertise.

Impact goals and results: Saie is on a mission to recover over 5 million pounds of plastic waste in three years (goal framed elsewhere as recovering 5 million lbs by 2027). As of May 2025, Saie had recovered 581,313 lbs of plastic waste. Since 2021, Saie has worked to remove twice as much plastic waste from the planet as it creates.

Community projects: Saie supports impact projects in Buenaventura, Colombia; Douala, Cameroon; Medellín, Colombia; Kerala, India; and West Java, Indonesia. Through rePurpose Impact Projects, hard-to-recycle plastics are repurposed into homes, schools, and playgrounds, and 50K people are said to gain access to structured waste management, improving health, sanitation, and livelihoods.

Women's empowerment: The Saie Climate Initiative empowers over 2,000 female waste workers with fair wages, healthcare, and social security through recovery efforts. Saie formed an all-women advisory board that includes rePurpose Global co-founder Svanika Balasubramanian.
