**What is Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR)?**
Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) is an environmental regulatory framework that makes brands responsible for the packaging they put into the world. Under EPR, companies must collect, format, and submit packaging data to each relevant state, then pay fees based on the types and amounts of packaging they use. First adopted in Europe and Canada, EPR was designed to shift the financial burden of waste management from the public sector to producers. All fees will go towards funding state-run recycling programs to improve waste collection infrastructure.
