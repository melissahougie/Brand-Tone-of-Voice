## Global Impact Standards / methodology (likely a linked or adjacent page — paraphrased from search summary)
- Framing line: "Plastic recovery had no universally accepted standards, so rePurpose Global built what became the global standard. Every plastic recovery claim they issue is backed by frameworks that guarantee measurable impact."
- Three governing documents referenced: the **Verified Plastic Recovery Protocol (VPRP)**, the **Impact Code**, and the **rePurpose Audit and Assurance Protocol (rAAP)**.
- VPRP requires each project to demonstrate: additionality, measurability, material traceability, uniqueness, verifiability, environmental safeguards, and socio-economic benefit.
- **Impact Code**: described as designed "to put waste workers and communities at the forefront" of the work to end the plastic crisis, outlining principles for collaboration with impact partners.
- **reTrace**: proprietary platform that logs photo, time, and GPS data at each step of the recovery/processing chain; paired with biannual internal audits and annual third-party audits for "full visibility from recovery through processing."

### BASE framework (inconsistent acronym expansion found across search results — flagging discrepancy)
Two different expansions of "BASE" appeared across searches and should be verified against the live page:
- Version A: **B**aseline, **A**dditionality, **S**ocial impact, **E**nvironmental integrity — described as "four pillars" ensuring VPRUs (Verified Plastic Recovery Units) "go beyond plastic recovery" into multidimensional impact.
- Version B: **B**aseline, **A**dditionality, **S**olution, **E**valuation — described in a separate blog-series context ("BASE: A foundation for impact on plastic waste," a 4-part blog series) as the framework used to develop Verified Plastic Recovery (VPR) solutions that "fix broken waste management value chains in under-served regions... by channeling finance to where it's needed."

Pillar descriptions found (paraphrased):
- **Baseline** — establishing clear starting points by measuring existing waste flows and collection infrastructure before intervention.
- **Additionality** — proving recovered plastic would not have been collected without the program, ensuring genuine incremental impact.
- **Social impact** — projects operate under the Impact Code, including fair wages, safe working conditions, inclusive employment.
- **Environmental integrity / Evaluation** — measuring outcomes against baselines via third-party verification and continuous monitoring.
