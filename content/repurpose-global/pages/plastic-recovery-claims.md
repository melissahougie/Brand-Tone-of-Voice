**What is Verified Plastic Recovery (VPR)?**
Verified Plastic Recovery is the ethical collection, removal, and recovery of plastic waste from the natural environment using a method that tracks and verifies the entire process. VPR focuses on low-value plastics that would otherwise become pollution—materials where the cost of collecting and processing is higher than their market value. This includes hard-to-recycle plastics like multi-layered packaging and flexible films that often end up illegally dumped, burned, or polluting ecosystems.
