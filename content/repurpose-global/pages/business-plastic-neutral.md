## /plasticneutralstandards ("Explore Our Plastic Neutral Standards")

### Definition (paraphrased)
"Plastic neutrality is the concept of measuring, reducing, and offsetting a company's unique plastic footprint," achieved when a company's plastic footprint "is measured and balanced by the removal and recovery of plastic waste from nature, and complemented by reductions in plastic use."

### Three-step certification process (paraphrased, but with specific figures that read as likely accurate/near-verbatim)
1. **Measure Plastic Footprint** — companies work with rePurpose sustainability experts to measure "the unique plastic footprint of their packaging and supply chains, using easily available data of product specs and sales stats."
2. **Fund Plastic Recovery** — "At $0.50 per kilogram of its footprint, each company funds the removal and reuse of an equivalent amount of low-value plastic waste otherwise too unprofitable to be diverted from landfills, incineration, or oceans," via "rePurpose Global's global impact network of vetted recycling initiatives across 3 continents."
3. **Commit to Reduction** — brands "commit to tangibly reduce their use of virgin plastic going forward."

### What certification means (near-verbatim)
"If a company is Plastic Neutral Certified, it means that for every pound of plastic they use, rePurpose Global removes an equivalent amount of additional, verified plastic waste from the environment through its partners."
