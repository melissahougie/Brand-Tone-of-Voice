# rePurpose Brand Reference

## Positioning (locked, approved)

> rePurpose takes packaging compliance completely off your plate — from all-state reporting to source reduction strategy — more accurately and faster than any other platform or consultant.

**Core promise (plain language):** You send us your data. We've got you 100% covered.

## Differentiator
Flat annual fee. No per-state charges. No per-report charges. No surprises. Tech scalability and cost efficiency vs. consultants.

## Geographic scope
North America EPR compliance (U.S. + Canada). Footer and trust lines should reference "North America," not just "U.S."

## Audience
EPR compliance professionals at brands producing packaged goods. Functions: Packaging, Regulatory, Compliance, Legal, EPR, Sustainability, Operations, Supply Chain, Innovation, R&D. Titles: Director, VP, Head of, Manager, Specialist.

Two psychographic profiles:

1. **The deeply in-it specialist.** Already working on compliance. Often a "one-person compliance team." Knows the regs. Looking for help executing.
2. **The recently-assigned generalist.** Someone (legal, ops, packaging) who just got handed EPR as another responsibility. Learning the regs in real time.

Both want calm, specific, expert help. Neither wants marketing copy or alarm bells.

## Climbing metaphor system
The Base Camp to Summit campaign establishes a recurring set of metaphors used throughout 2026 content.

- **Base Camp** = where you start
- **Camp 1** = Data Check
- **Camp 2** = State Mapping
- **Camp 3** = California Focus
- **Camp 4** = Fee Review
- **Camp 5** = Final Review
- **Summit** = May 31 (or the deadline at hand)

Recurring phrases that are part of the system:
- "Heavy pack? We've got it." (closing line)
- "We've made this climb with 150+ brands"
- "You don't have to summit alone"
- "Same trail, different starting point" (for late-comers)
- "Join the climb" / "Don't climb alone" (CTAs)
- "The trail is still walkable"

## Sign-offs by use case
- **Prospect and lead emails:** "Heavy pack? We've got it."
- **Customer emails:** "Here for you." or "We've Got You Covered."
- **Regulatory Roundup (weekly customer newsletter, from compliance.updates@repurpose.global):** "We've Got You Covered."
- **Casual emails (Office Hours, follow-ups):** "Here for you." or context-specific.

## Approved claims (verified, OK to use)
- "150+ brands across North America trust us with their EPR"
- "Reporting-ready in 2 weeks" (from data collection)
- "Flat annual fee, no surprises"
- "Software backed by experts"
- "We've handled every filing, every fee forecast, and every regulatory curveball"
- Tillamook: $100k saved on Oregon EPR fees through accurate material reclassification; 4-5 hours per week reclaimed
- Ancient Nutrition: scaled EPR compliance without adding headcount
- Public ARR framing: "Quadrupled new product line ARR in the first half of 2026"

## Claims to avoid or flag
- **"#1 Packaging Compliance Platform"** — appears on the rePurpose website but is NOT in the approved positioning from Naomi. Flag inconsistency when it comes up.
- **"From $0 to $10M ARR in under a year"** — inaccurate (combines VPR + EPR product lines). Do not use.

## Things we don't say
- **"Random acts of marketing"** — Emily Kramer's phrase, not ours.
- **"Influencers"** — use "creators" for any creator-economy or partnership context.
- **"Disrupt" or "disruption"** — overused, vague, doesn't match our voice.
- **Industry-jargon clusters** that exclude the recently-assigned generalist reader.
- **Fear-language** — even when stakes are real, the tone is "we've got you," not "panic."
